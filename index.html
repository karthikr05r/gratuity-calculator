01

What is gratuity?

Gratuity is a lump-sum benefit an eligible employee may receive from an employer when leaving employment, subject to applicable rules.

02

How is it estimated?

For employees covered by the common formula, the estimate is: Last drawn Basic + DA × 15 ÷ 26 × eligible years of service.

03

Check your eligibility

Eligibility and the applicable formula may depend on employment type, length of service, and current law. Check with HR for your situation.
